CANDIDATE INSTRUCTIONS

Strict exam-day rules

Read before starting

JEE-style demo instructions. These are not official NTA/JEE rules; follow the instructions issued for your actual examination.

  1. Remain present: Stay at your desk and in camera view throughout the test. Keep your face visible, lighting adequate, and the room clear of other people.
  2. No assistance: Do not communicate with anyone, receive help, or use unauthorized notes, books, devices, or tools. Use a calculator only if your examiner explicitly permits it.
  3. Stay on the exam: Do not switch tabs or apps, minimize the exam, refresh the page, or navigate away. Focus changes may be recorded; closing or leaving the exam before completion marks the session as left.
  4. Protect exam content: Do not copy, photograph, record, publish, or share questions or answers.
  5. Keep the setup ready: Use a stable internet connection and power source. Do not cover or disconnect the camera while the exam is running.
  6. Follow examiner directions: Respond to permitted on-screen requests. Camera video sharing is separate from the on-device check and is shared only with your consent. This demo does not use your microphone.
