Simple checks.

The camera check runs on your device. It looks for a face; it does not identify you. You choose whether an examiner tab in this browser can watch your video.

This demo shares exam status and possible alerts between tabs in the same browser. It does not connect to a remote examiner. Alerts can be wrong, so an examiner should review them before acting.
